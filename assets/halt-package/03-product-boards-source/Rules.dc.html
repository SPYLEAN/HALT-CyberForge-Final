03 — CLEAR SPACE, MINIMUM SIZE, MISUSE

Keep the cut readable

CLEAR SPACE · 1.5x ON ALL SIDES, x = STEM WIDTH

x = 16 grid units, the width of one stem. The same 1.5x applies to the icon, stacked lockup and wordmark. No text, edge or other graphic enters the dashed zone.

Below 24 px the full cut is replaced by the favicon cut: thicker stem, solid node, wider gap, no chamfer. Never scale the full cut down to fit a 16 px slot.
BACKGROUND
Ink or Bone on solid grounds, 7:1 contrast or better. Use the Bone mark on Ink and the Ink mark on Bone or white. On photography, place the mark on a flat ground first.
INCORRECT USE
Do not enclose the mark in a shield, lock, badge or container.
Do not close the cut. The gap and the node are the idea.
Do not swap role colours. Ember is hostile, Verdigris is business, never the reverse.
Do not rotate, skew or tilt. The H stays upright and orthogonal.
Do not add glow, gradients, shadows or neon colour.
Do not stretch, squash or change proportions.
Do not place on low-contrast or busy grounds. Keep 7:1.
Halt
Do not retype the wordmark in a font. Use the drawn HALT artwork only.
