05 — GRAPH LANGUAGE

The mark, unfolded into a network

Every graph element is one part of the H. If a shape on screen cannot be traced back to the mark, it does not belong in the graph.
Asset node

Ring, Ø 1x, hole 0.37x. A host, service or identity. Always Bone.

Edge

Orthogonal runs with 45° corner cuts only. Never curves. Weight 0.5x in graphs, 1x as hero.

Business path

Verdigris, straight and unbroken. Runs past the nodes it serves, like the spine.

Hostile path

Ember. Starts at a chamfered entry that shows the direction it came from.

Cut and verification

Edge, gap 0.22x, node, gap, edge. The node is where the proof is checked.

Held segment

Ember at 35%. The part of the attack path that can no longer be reached.

COMPOSITION · ONE CUT, BUSINESS UNBROKEN
GEOMETRY

One grid, x = 16 units. Layouts snap to 2x. Corners are 45° cuts of 0.5x. The cut gap is 0.22x either side of a node, the same ratio as in the mark.

COLOUR AS MEANING

Ember means hostile, Verdigris means business, Bone means node and proof. Nothing else is coloured. A graph with no hostile path is calm: Verdigris and Bone on Ink.

DENSITY

At scale, edges thin to 0.25x and nodes drop their hole above 200 nodes, mirroring the favicon cut. The cut and its verification node stay full size at every zoom.
