Semantic mapping · themes switch with the page

Page background

--bg

Ground. Everything else is a step off it.

Quiet ground lets the two threads be the loudest things on the page.

Elevated surface

--surface-raised

Drawers, dialogs, tooltips, toasts. With a 1px ring.

Separation without shadow: nothing floats over the business thread.

Primary text

--text

Headlines, body, data values.

Evidence first: readable at 13.8:1 or more.

Secondary text

--text-secondary

Labels, IDs, derivations.

Derivations always visible, never faint: 6:1 or more.

Observed threat

--threat-observed

Solid hostile thread, evidence marker, attacker node.

The attack, shown only where evidence exists.

Forecast threat

--threat-forecast

Same hue as observed; told apart by dotted stroke and dashed ring.

Uncertain futures never look like facts.

Business continuity

--business

Preserved thread, dependency edge, healthy process.

The thing HALT promises not to stop.

Verified proof

--proof-ring

Proof ring with business-coloured centre.

A verified claim is a claim about preserved business operation.

Warning

--warning

Triangle glyph, CAUTION, dashed border.

Never confusable with hostile or healthy.

Success

--success

Check glyph and word: APPLIED, VERIFIED.

Success means the business kept running.

Neutral evidence

--neutral-evidence

Inferred edges, evidence marker outlines.

What HALT infers is not what it observed.

Disabled state

--text-disabled

Disabled labels with a stated reason.

Execute is disabled until both humans approve.

Four brand colours (Ink, Bone, Ember, Verdigris) plus a neutral ramp carry all twelve roles. No other hue exists. Warning and Success add no colour: Warning is the text colour with a triangle and a dashed border, Success is Verdigris with a check and a word.
