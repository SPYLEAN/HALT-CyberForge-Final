The containment paradox

A response can stop the attacker—and the organisation.

The obvious answer is to isolate everything the attacker could reach. Here that means the finance estate. The attacker loses access. So do the people who close the books, run payroll and reconcile the ledger.

Two kinds of damage appear on the same incident. HALT keeps them apart, because only one of them is the attacker’s doing.
